Kalyana Joins the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program

Kalyana has joined the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program, supporting our work across enterprise AI, Microsoft cloud, data, and automation.

Kalyana Solutions / November 28, 2025 / 3 min read

Kalyana has joined the Microsoft AI Cloud Partner Program.

Partnership announcements can be vague, so here is what this one means for our work. Our team gets better access to Microsoft product resources, technical support, and training. We will use that access in the projects we already deliver: enterprise AI, data and reporting, automation, and integration with core business systems.

Where Microsoft fits

Many of our clients already run important work on Microsoft 365, Azure, Power BI, or Power Automate. The opportunity is rarely to add another isolated tool. It is to connect those platforms to the process around them.

That might mean grounding an enterprise agent in approved Microsoft 365 content, building an AI application on Azure, preparing data in Fabric for operational reporting, or using Power Automate alongside RPA and APIs. Microsoft 365 Copilot is part of the portfolio, but it is not the whole Enterprise AI story.

We will continue to choose the architecture around the job. Microsoft technology may provide the model, data platform, workflow, identity, or application surface. Other platforms may sit beside it. The process, controls, and support model still have to work as one system.

Looking at an Azure, Microsoft 365, Power Platform, or Enterprise AI project? Talk to Kalyana.
